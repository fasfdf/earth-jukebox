Earth Jukebox

Pick a NASA view of our changing planet and listen to it. Sound is generated live, in step with the picture, so the science can be heard as well as seen.

How to listen

  1. Choose a visualization, then press Play. Nothing plays until you do.
  2. Listen for pitch first: a higher note means a higher value. The table called “What you are hearing” lists everything else.
  3. Items under From NASA measurements turn the measured numbers into sound: every note is a real value. Start with these.
  4. Items under From video colors turn the colors of a NASA animation into sound. They let you explore a visualization by ear, but color is only a picture of the data, not the measurement.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls, Space plays or pauses, and the arrow keys move the timeline when it has focus.

      Light to sound

      Light and sound are both waves, but visible light vibrates about a trillion times faster than anything we can hear. Halve a light frequency 40 times (drop it 40 octaves) and it lands in the range of a piano, with the spacing between colors kept intact.

      This is a way to explore the math, not a measurement. The swatch color is approximate.
